My Journey & Purpose

From a Tier-3 College to Direct PhD at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

A story of relentless grit, self-driven ethical curiosity, and proving that perseverance beats privilege every single time.

Keynote at E-Cell IIT Tirupati The IdeaGen Technologies Presents ASPIRE · Empowering Horizons

"Safeguarding the digital world shouldn’t be an elite club; it should be a path anyone can walk if given the chance, tools, and a mentor who’s been there."

— Sainath Reddy Akkala

The Journey of mine

I grew up in a tier-3 college where hacking resources were scarce and opportunities felt miles away — so I made them. Between late nights and grinding second-shift hours, I taught myself networking, ethical hacking, and system exploitation from whatever scraps of information I could find, driven by pure curiosity and a stubborn refusal to let limited means define my limits.

That grit turned into results: Hall of Fame spots at the World Health Organization (WHO), SAP, and IISc Bangalore; corporate appreciation from LG Electronics and Britannia Industries; sovereign security acknowledgements from the Government of India (15+ NCIIPC letters), the Government of Nigeria (NITDA-CERRT), and IIT Tirupati; alongside a Special Mention Award at IKMC (IKP Knowledge Park) — each one a milestone that proved perseverance beats privilege.

What fuels me is the conviction that safeguarding the digital world shouldn’t be an elite club; it should be a path anyone can walk if given the chance, tools, and a mentor who’s been there. Today, standing at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad pursuing my Direct PhD in Information Security with my tier-3 B.Tech degree, my goal is to help all the students like me who had missed the dream IIT or NIT and joined a tier-3 college and are struggling with guidance and resources. I have been where you are — and I am here.

IEEE APSCON 2026 · IIT Delhi Best Paper Award 2026

Multi-Sensor Fusion Framework for UAV Navigation in GPS-Denied Environment

Proposes a fault-tolerant multi-sensor fusion architecture synthesizing 3D LiDAR, stereo vision, and high-rate IMU data to maintain accurate autonomous UAV trajectory tracking during complete GNSS outages or intentional spoofing/jamming attacks.

View Paper
IEEE INDICON 2025 · IIIT Bangalore 2025

Resilient Navigation for UAVs: GNSS Attack Detection & Seamless Transition to Visual-Inertial Localization

Develops statistical anomaly detection filters that identify GNSS spoofing signatures and orchestrate a zero-latency failover to visual-inertial odometry (VIO), preventing aerial vehicle hijacking and preserving navigation safety in contested zones.

View Paper
IEEE IITCEE 2026 · BMIT Bangalore 2026

A Modular, Passive Scanning Framework for Efficient Web Vulnerability Enumeration and Comprehensive Security Analysis

Presents an automated, low-footprint passive scanning engine capable of identifying and classifying P3–P5 risk tier vulnerabilities without inducing operational overhead or tripping intrusion detection systems (IDS/WAF).
